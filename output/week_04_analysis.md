# TRADES & FREE AGENTS

## The waiver situation first

**This league is FAAB, not priority** — $100 for the season, and you have spent **$0**, so all $100
is live. Your waiver position is **5 of 10**, and under FAAB that position is **only the tiebreaker
between equal bids**. It does not gate anything: the only thing that stops you winning a claim is
being outbid.

The field is wide open. You are **tied for the largest budget with five other teams**, **five of
nine rivals have spent nothing at all**, and the largest spend by anyone in the league is $16
(OprahWinfrey, $84 left). Nobody has committed to anything yet.

About **11 weekly waiver runs** remain before playoffs start in week 15 — an even pace of **$9 per
week**. Go well above that for a genuine starter, well below for a stream. Minimum bid is **$0**, so
an uncontested add is free, but a $0 bid loses every tie and ties go to waiver position, where you
sit mid-pack. So: **avoid round numbers.** $6 beats $5, and most managers bid in fives. Waivers
appear to run Tuesday with a 2-day claim window.
