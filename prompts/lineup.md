# Weekly Lineup Prompt Template

Copy the contents of your `output/week_XX.md` file, then paste this prompt after it:

---

Review my Week [X] fantasy football lineup. Based on the stats, matchups, and injury info above:

1. **Start/Sit:** Are my current starters correct? Flag any bench players I should start over a current starter and explain why.

2. **Risk Alerts:** Any players with concerning trends, tough matchups, or injury risk I should watch?

3. **Waiver Targets:** Based on my roster gaps, suggest 2-3 players I should look for on the waiver wire. Focus on high-upside adds that fill a real need.

4. **FLEX Decision:** Who is my best FLEX play this week and why?

Keep the analysis concise. Lead with the moves, then the reasoning.
